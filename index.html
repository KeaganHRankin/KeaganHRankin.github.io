Keagan Hudson Rankin

PhD candidate & Vanier Scholar · University of Toronto Engineering

My research investigates the resource demands of future human development. I think about how we can design material & energy systems that meet future demand within planetary limits. I'm also interested in epistemic questions r.e. how we use and interpret computational models (e.g. machine learning, energy system optimizations, stochastic simulations) in applied sciences. I spent part of my PhD at the University of Cambridge Centre for Sustainable Development, and before Toronto I completed a BScEng at the University of New Brunswick.

Research and selected papers

All papers →

My primary doctoral work is supervised by Shoshanna Saxe and Daniel Posen. Our research has helped inform housing and zoning policy in the City of Toronto and other municipalities across Canada. We won national research award from the Canadian Federal Government in 2024 for our research on climate-compatible housing. Our work has been covered by media outlets like Forbes.

I'm also involved in a few collaborations with international colleagues. Several ISIE members and I are researching how rapid developments in AI are affecting the reproducibility of sustainabiltiy science and life cycle assessment. I'm also working with the IRP at the United Nations EP to model the possible growth pathways, and environmental impacts, of cities in developing countries.
